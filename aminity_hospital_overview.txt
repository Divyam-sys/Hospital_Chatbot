1 HOSPITAL OVERVIEW
________________________________________________________________________________
Aminity Hospital is a premier 650 bed quaternary care tertiary academic medical 
center dedicated to providing compassionate patient centered and state of the art 
healthcare Founded on the principles of medical excellence clinical research 
and community service Aminity Hospital serves over 120000 inpatients and 
450000 outpatients annually across 35 specialized departments

2 HISTORY AND EXPANSION TIMELINE
________________________________________________________________________________
  1998 Founded by Dr Arthur Vance and the Aminity Healthcare Foundation as a 100 bed community hospital focusing on internal medicine and trauma care
  2004 Expanded to 250 beds with the addition of the Arthur Vance Heart and Vascular Center
  2010 Received International Joint Commission JCI accreditation and established the Neuroscience Center of Excellence
  2015 Constructed the 10 story East Wing increasing capacity to 500 beds and integrating advanced robotic surgery suites
  2021 Inaugurated the Aminity Comprehensive Cancer Center and Pediatric ICU expanding total bed capacity to 650
  2024 Launched the Digital Health and AI Diagnostics Center pioneering personalized genomic medicine

1 GENERAL HOSPITAL VISITATION RULES
________________________________________________________________________________
  Visiting Hours General Wards 10 00 AM to 12 00 PM and 04 00 PM to 07 00 PM daily
  ICU Visiting Hours 11 00 AM to 12 00 PM and 05 00 PM to 06 00 PM restricted to immediate family members only
  Visitor Passes Every visitor must obtain a valid Visitor Pass at the security desk on Floor 1
  Maximum Visitors Maximum of 2 visitors per patient at any given time in general wards and 1 visitor in ICU
  Age Restrictions Children under the age of 12 are not permitted in ICU CCU and Isolation Wards for infection control reasons

2 INFECTION CONTROL AND HYGIENE MANDATES
________________________________________________________________________________
  Hand Hygiene Mandatory hand sanitization using wall mounted dispensers before entering and exiting any patient room
  Mask Policy Surgical masks must be worn at all times inside high risk areas including ICUs Oncology wards and Emergency Departments
  Outside Food Policy Outside cooked food is strictly prohibited inside patient rooms Custom nutritional meals are provided by the hospital dietary department
  Flowers and Plants Live flowers and potted plants are banned in all inpatient units due to bacterial and fungal risks

3 PATIENT RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES
________________________________________________________________________________
  Right to Information Patients have the right to receive full information regarding diagnosis treatment options and estimated costs
  Patient Identity Tags Hospital issued ID wristbands must be worn by patients at all times during their stay
  Personal Belongings The hospital is not responsible for the loss of valuable items jewelry or cash Patients are advised to keep personal items to a minimum

4 SAFETY SECURITY AND PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
________________________________________________________________________________
  Zero Tolerance Smoke Free Policy Smoking vaping or tobacco consumption is strictly banned anywhere within the hospital campus including open parking lots
  Alcohol and Illegal Substances Alcohol possession or consumption is strictly prohibited Offenders will be reported to law enforcement immediately
  Zero Tolerance for Violence Physical aggression verbal abuse or harassment directed at medical staff nurses or employees will result in immediate security removal and legal action
  Photography and Video Recording To protect patient privacy and confidentiality recording video or taking photos of patients staff or equipment without written consent is forbidden

5 ADMISSION DISCHARGE AND PAYMENT POLICIES
________________________________________________________________________________
  Advance Deposit A baseline deposit is required at the time of elective admission as per the procedural financial schedule
  Discharge Clearance Patients will only be discharged after complete clinical clearance by the attending consultant and financial settlement at the billing desk
  Quiet Hours Strict quiet hours are enforced from 10 00 PM to 06 00 AM to facilitate patient rest Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during this period

2 SPECIALIZED NURSING EXCELLENCE AND ROLES
________________________________________________________________________________
Aminity Hospital employs over 1400 registered nurses under a Magnet certified 
care model Nursing specialties include

  Advanced Practice Registered Nurses APRNs and Nurse Practitioners NPs Independently manage patient care write prescriptions and lead outpatient clinics
  Critical Care Nurses CCRN Provide high acuity care in ICUs managing mechanical ventilation ECMO and continuous renal replacement therapy CRRT
  Perioperative / OR Nurses CNOR Specialize in surgical scrub circulating and sterile field maintenance for complex operative procedures
  Clinical Nurse Specialists CNS Lead quality improvement evidence based nursing protocols and clinical education across wards

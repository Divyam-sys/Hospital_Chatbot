2 WHAT TO DO IN SPECIFIC MEDICAL EMERGENCIES
________________________________________________________________________________

A HEART ATTACK CHEST PAIN
     Symptoms Crushing chest pain radiation to jaw/left arm shortness of breath cold sweat
     Immediate Actions
     1 Call emergency services +1 800 555 9111 immediately Do NOT drive yourself
     2 Keep the person calm seated or resting in a semi upright position
     3 Chewing 325mg of uncoated aspirin if not allergic and advised by emergency dispatchers
     4 If unconscious and not breathing begin CPR at 100 to 120 compressions per minute immediately

B STROKE FAST PROTOCOL
     Watch for FAST Signs
       F Face drooping Ask person to smile
       A Arm weakness Ask person to raise both arms
       S Speech difficulty Ask person to repeat a simple sentence
       T Time to call emergency Call emergency hotline immediately
     Immediate Actions Note the EXACT time symptoms started Do NOT give food drink or medication

C SEVERE TRAUMA AND BLEEDING
     Immediate Actions
     1 Apply direct firm pressure to the bleeding wound using a clean cloth or sterile bandage
     2 Keep pressure elevated above heart level if possible
     3 Do NOT remove embedded foreign objects stabilize them with bulky padding around the site
     4 Keep the victim warm with blankets to prevent shock

D SEVERE BURNS
     Immediate Actions
     1 Cool the burn immediately with cool not ice cold running water for at least 10 to 15 minutes
     2 Do NOT apply ice butter or ointments to open burns
     3 Cover loosely with a clean dry sterile non stick bandage
     4 Transport immediately to the Aminity Hospital Burn and Trauma Unit

3 TRIAGE CATEGORIZATION AND EMERGENCY ADMISSION
________________________________________________________________________________
Upon arrival at Aminity Hospital ER all patients are immediately evaluated by a 
Triage Nurse and assigned a clinical urgency category

  Level 1 Resuscitation / Immediate Life threatening emergencies e g Cardiac arrest massive trauma respiratory failure Immediate care
  Level 2 Emergency / Under 10 Mins Critical conditions e g Severe chest pain stroke symptoms major fractures
  Level 3 Urgent / Under 30 Mins Serious condition requiring multiple tests e g Moderate abdominal pain high fever with severe lethargy
  Level 4 Semi Urgent Non life threatening conditions e g Minor laceration needing sutures simple sprain
  Level 5 Non Urgent Low acuity e g Prescription refill mild rash
